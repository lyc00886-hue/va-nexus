# VA-Nexus Privacy Notice (MVP trial)

**Guest mode**
- Answers from the learning support check-in and SEN display settings are stored only in your device's browser (localStorage). They are not uploaded and do not sync across devices.
- Guests can use lessons and games, but cannot upload SBA photos. Progress and badges stay on this device. If you clear your browser data or switch to another device, these records will be lost.

**After Google sign-in**
- Game progress and badges sync with your own account, so they are not lost when you change devices.
- SBA photos sync and are kept in private storage that only you can see (each account can only read and write its own files).
- SEN display settings and check-in answers still stay on this device only. They do not sync to your account, and teachers or schools cannot see them.
- We will not sell your data to anyone, and we will not use it for advertising.
- The AI feedback feature sends only the art criticism text you write to Google Gemini for processing. Please do not include personal details such as names or student numbers.
- You can ask us to delete your account and all of your data at any time.

If you have questions, please contact your teacher.
