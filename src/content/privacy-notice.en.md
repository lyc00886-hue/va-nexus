# VA-Nexus Privacy Notice (Trial Version)

**Guest mode**
- Answers from the learning-style check-in and display settings are stored only in this device's browser (localStorage). They are not uploaded and do not sync across devices.
- The check-in and display settings only help you choose a more comfortable interface (for example larger text, read-aloud, or fewer animations). They are not a diagnosis and do not label or decide whether you have any learning need.
- Guests can use lessons and games but cannot upload SBA photos. Progress and badges stay on this device. If you clear browser data or switch devices, these records will be lost.

**After signing in with Google**
- Game progress and badges sync to your own account, so they are kept when you change devices.
- SBA private notes and photos stay in the student's own device / account private storage that only you can see (each account can only read and write its own files).
- Teacher marking records are kept privately on Drive only. They are not made public and are never sent to AI.
- Display settings and check-in answers still stay on this device only. They do not sync to your account, and teachers or the school cannot see them.

**AI feedback (18+ accounts only)**
- AI feedback is available only to accounts marked as 18 or older. Under-18 users can use guides, checklists and sentence frames, but not AI.
- AI feedback uses Google Gemini. Student writing is sent to Gemini only through our server. The browser never calls Gemini directly, and the API key never leaves the server.
- This is a paid Gemini service (billed to the school / teacher account). Under Google's API terms, student prompts and responses are not used to train Google's AI models.
- Only when you tap "Get feedback" is that criticism text sent. Nothing is sent unless you tap. Please do not include personal details such as names or student numbers.
- Teacher marking records, display settings, check-in answers, and SBA private notes / photos are never sent to AI.
- AI feedback is for learning only. It is not an official mark, and AI does not mark SBA work.

**Ability charts**
- Ability charts show only your own four-level self-assessment. They do not compare you with classmates and do not rank anyone.
- Every chart states that it is for self-review only and is not an official SBA mark; official marks are given by your subject teacher.
- Ability charts never show any diagnostic label or diagnosis.

**Other**
- We will not sell your data to anyone, and we will not use it for advertising.
- You can ask us to delete your account and all of your data at any time.

If you have questions, please contact your teacher.
